# Musa AI Video Factory — Quality Standard

A successful run is not automatically publish-ready. Every deliverable must pass editorial and technical review.

## Editorial
- Hook in the first 1–2 seconds; one clear promise or question.
- Narrative arc: hook → context → explanation/action → payoff → memorable ending.
- Every shot adds new information. No repeated stills, filler zooms or duplicate compositions.
- Conversational, accurate narration paced to the visual beat.
- Concise, proofread on-screen text inside mobile safe areas.

## Visuals
- Create a character/subject bible: silhouette, face, proportions, wardrobe, palette, materials and distinguishing features.
- Reuse reference images when supported; repeat continuity rules in every scene.
- Every scene gets a distinct action, composition, framing and camera move.
- Favor plausible motion, lighting, contact shadows, reflections and scale.
- Reject malformed faces/hands, temporal flicker, morphing, floating objects, unreadable text, logos and watermarks.
- A still with a zoom is not a substitute for animated footage.

## Audio
- Narration must remain intelligible above music and effects.
- Use original, licensed or user-provided music/SFX only; record source and license.
- Duck music under speech; avoid clipping and sudden loudness changes.
- Do not imitate a living artist or clone a voice without authorization.

## Exports
- TikTok / Reels / Shorts: 9:16, 1080×1920, H.264 MP4, AAC audio, 30 fps delivery target.
- Keep key text/action clear of UI overlays and edges.
- Create platform-specific captions, hashtags and thumbnail text. Never auto-publish without approval.
- Keep a clean master and derive platform versions from it.

## Final QA
- [ ] Hook works without sound; story works with captions.
- [ ] No placeholder/repeated scenes; continuity reviewed.
- [ ] Genuine temporal motion; no obvious visual artifacts.
- [ ] Voice, music and SFX balanced; captions timed and proofread.
- [ ] Asset rights checked; export verified with ffprobe.
- [ ] Watch the full video before publishing.
